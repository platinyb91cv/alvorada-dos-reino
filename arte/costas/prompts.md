# Prompts — vista de costas das unidades (Gemini)

Estilo e regras iguais às do rei e dos edifícios. Cola primeiro o bloco **REGRAS**
(numa conversa nova) e depois um prompt de unidade de cada vez. Cada unidade fica
de pé, direita, pés juntos e braços em baixo (pose base — o jogo anima as pernas).

A roupa da equipa é **ROXA** e tem de ser o único roxo forte da imagem (é a parte
que muda de cor conforme o jogador).

---

## REGRAS (colar uma vez)

From now, generate single game-character sprites for a medieval RTS, painted
semi-realistic Age of Empires II unit style. RULES for every image: ONE character
only, centered, full body, on a flat solid pure magenta #FF00FF background — nothing
else, no ground, no grass, no shadow on the background, no text, sharp clean edges,
no magenta tint on the character. High 3/4 top-down ISOMETRIC view (camera about 45°
above), looking at the character strictly FROM BEHIND — his BACK turned to the viewer,
NO face visible. Pose: standing straight, feet together, both arms down at the sides.
The team cloth is ROYAL PURPLE and must be clearly visible on his back, and it must be
the only strong purple in the image.

---

## Prompts por unidade

**guerreiro** — Next image, same rules and style: a foot soldier (man-at-arms) seen from
behind — rounded iron kettle helmet, knee-length tunic with a PURPLE surcoat over
chainmail, brown leather belt, leather boots, a round wooden shield on the left arm, a
sheathed short sword at the hip.

**espadachim** — Next image, same rules and style: a heavy swordsman seen from behind —
iron helmet, chainmail hauberk with a PURPLE surcoat, steel shoulder plates, leather
boots, a round shield on his back, a long sword held point-down at his right side.

**lanceiro** — Next image, same rules and style: a spearman seen from behind — simple
leather cap, PURPLE tunic over padded armor, leather boots, a tall spear held upright in
one hand, a small round shield on his back.

**arqueiro** — Next image, same rules and style: an archer seen from behind — leather cap
or hood, PURPLE tunic, leather bracers, a wooden longbow held in one hand, and a QUIVER
of arrows clearly visible on his back.

**besteiro** — Next image, same rules and style: a crossbowman seen from behind — iron
cap, PURPLE tabard over a gambeson, a wooden crossbow held down at his side, a quiver of
bolts on his back.

**sacerdote** — Next image, same rules and style: a monk/priest seen from behind — a long
hooded robe in deep PURPLE with cream trim, hood up, holding a rolled scroll; no armor,
no weapon.

**cavaleiro** — Next image, same rules and style: a mounted knight seen strictly FROM
BEHIND — a brown warhorse standing and facing away (we see its hindquarters and tail),
the rider on top in chainmail with a PURPLE surcoat and a helmet, a PURPLE caparison over
the horse's rump, a lance held upright. Whole horse-and-rider centered on the magenta.

**cavArqueira** — Next image, same rules and style: a mounted horse-archer seen strictly
FROM BEHIND — a brown horse facing away (hindquarters and tail toward the viewer), the
rider in a PURPLE tunic with a bow in hand and a quiver on the back.

**aldeao** — Next image, same rules and style: a peasant villager seen from behind — no
helmet, short brown hair, a simple PURPLE-and-cream tunic, brown trousers, leather shoes,
empty hands at the sides, unarmored and humble.

---

## Notas

- A cavalaria (cavaleiro, cavArqueira) é a mais difícil: a traseira do cavalo pode sair
  estranha. Se ficar mau, repete o prompt com "the horse seen directly from behind,
  symmetric, tail in the center".
- Depois de cada imagem, eu recorto o magenta, meço os pés e meto no jogo (atlas ANIMB).
  Basta a pose base de pé; o jogo faz as pernas andar.
- Se o traço ficar diferente do da frente, acrescenta ao prompt: "match the exact same
  painting style, colors and proportions as the previous soldier".
